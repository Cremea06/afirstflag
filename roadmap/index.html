Roadmap

Milliway is the public room where the action starts. Over time, we want it to grow into a community platform for American-made goods, preparedness, local chapters, and patriotic communities.

Outsmart the algorithms, don’t become one.

Live now

Private whispers in Milliway (/tell and /r).

The ten pieces

  1. 1

    Identity

    Profiles with a tier badge, a flag number, and a join date.

    Inspired by every platform

  2. 2

    Live square

    Milliway as a curated stage that creators can book time on.

    Inspired by Twitch, Kick, Rumble

  3. 3

    Private rooms

    Invite-only or gated rooms, including fifty state Chapter rooms, starting with whispers.

    Inspired by Discord, Reddit, World of Warcraft

  4. 4

    Library

    Topic forums ranked by votes.

    Inspired by Reddit

  5. 5

    Feed

    Members pick or build their own feed.

    Inspired by X, TikTok, Bluesky

  6. 6

    Short clips

    Vertical highlights and saved streams.

    Inspired by TikTok, X, Twitch

  7. 7

    Creator economy

    Subscriptions, gifted subs, tips, booked slots, and a creator-first split.

    Inspired by Twitch, Kick, X

  8. 8

    Trust layer

    Reporting and blocking bad actors, community notes-style checks, and shared moderation lists.

    Inspired by X, Bluesky, Discord

  9. 9

    Commerce in stream

    Buy flags and partner products from chat, a stream, or a clip.

    Inspired by TikTok Shop, Instagram

  10. 10

    Portability

    Your own handle, and you can take your data with you.

    Inspired by Bluesky

How it could take shape

  1. Phase 0. Foundations

    Profiles, a privacy policy for private messages, and community rules.

  2. Phase 1. Social core

    Private rooms, a supporter tier with a chat badge, and the ability to block and report.

  3. Phase 2. Library

    A few seeded forums, with votes weighted toward verified members.

  4. Phase 3. Scheduled stage

    Creators book Milliway slots, and Tailgate follows the schedule.

  5. Phase 4. Economy

    Gifted subs, tips, creator payouts, and buy buttons in chat and stream.

  6. Phase 5. Feed and clips

    Member-chosen feeds, vertical clips, and replays.

  7. Phase 6. Trust and portability

    Fact checks, shared blocklists, custom handles, and data export.

Ideas we’re exploring

  • Save your stream after you go live, and download a copy to your own computer. A version that stays on the site would come later, if we build it.

Where we’re different

  • Pick a niche, not a fight. We would rather build a second home that is useful than try to replace a giant platform.
  • Verified, invested members. One flag, one vote.
  • Feeds members control. You choose what you see, instead of an algorithm choosing for you.
  • A creator-first deal. The people who show up and make the room worth visiting should keep the better share.
  • Creators own their content. What you make is yours.
  • Built on what exists. We start from the shop, the chat, and the tools already in place, and grow from there.

This is a living draft; nothing here is a promise or a schedule.
